Play 斗地主经典版 fast, one decision at a time. Only works while that game is on screen. One call does your move and then waits on the phone, without the model, until the next decision is yours. The result gives `phase`, `handCount` and the screenshot of the new decision. Each turn has a 15 second clock, so decide at once and do not call other tools during a game. `start` keeps the existing single-player behavior when `mode` is omitted or `single`; use `mode=human` explicitly for a human game, always with `advisor`. In human mode, pass `auto=true` to make `start` continuously drive call, double, lead, follow, pass, and DouZero play decisions inside the phone tool until a pause, result, or unsupported scene; do not wait for the model between decisions. In human mode, call `start` from the game main lobby (three cards: 单机模式, 真人对战, 好友约牌) or from the human room list, without tapping first: with `auto=true` it taps the center 真人对战 card and then the room by itself (`room`: `normal` is the green 普通场, the default; `elite` is the cyan 精英场; `tuhao` is the blue 土豪场, 1000万-3亿; `master` is the purple 大师场 on the second row, 2000万-6亿, the highest stakes), then waits up to about 45 seconds for matching and the table to appear so it can bid from the deal. While waiting, and during play, the tool clears the game's own popups itself: the relief grant (救济金: the free claim when there is one, otherwise the reward video), the reward video's ads (it never taps 立刻打开 or 直接拿奖励), the reward screen, and an ad end card over the table; after a relief claim it taps the same room again. 普通场 only seats players with up to 200万 beans: above that, tapping it shows 您的豆豆足够到高级的房间游戏啦; `start` itself closes that popup with its ✕ (never 立即前往) and returns error `beans_too_many`. Then call `start` again with `room=elite`, `room=tuhao` or `room=master` only if the user allows the higher stakes; otherwise stop and tell the user. 精英场, 土豪场 and 大师场 stakes are much higher. Do not handle those yourself. If the bottom of the matching screen or table names a different room than you asked for (for example it says 经典至尊场 when you asked for `master`), the tool never bids or plays there, stops, and returns error `room_mismatch` with the room it read in `room`: stop at once, do not pick another room, do not retry `start`, and report the mismatch to the user exactly as it is. On a human result screen (green 离开, blue 换桌, yellow 继续 at the bottom) do not tap any button yourself, not even when the result says 破产: call `start` with `mode=human` and `auto=true` again, and it taps the green 离开 itself and goes back into the room given by `room` through the usual lobby and room list; never tap 继续 or 换桌 there, 继续 can lead into the game store. On the main lobby always choose 真人对战 and never 单机模式; on the single-player 经典模式 room list it refuses with `single_player_rooms`. On the 商店 store page (opened on a cold start after going broke) `start` with `auto=true` taps only its back arrow itself; never buy anything there. If you must tap 真人对战 or a room yourself, first call `phone_observe` and tap only the text position it reports; when that text is not on screen, stop and hand the task back. Never estimate coordinates from a screenshot. If the result has error `letterboxed`, the game is in Android's small compatibility window and its controls are misaligned: tap the system's 重启应用以获得更好的显示 button (or reopen the game) so it fills the screen, then call `start` again. Without `auto=true`, `start` only attaches to the current table for inspection or recovery. In an existing human session, `start` is a recovery/idempotent call and must not reset the current game history. `stop` is idempotent and blocks later automatic work until a new `start`. `wait` drives automatic human-mode call, double, lead, follow, and pass decisions until the next visible decision, pause, result, or unsupported scene. Results return `phase`, `handCount`, `state` (`calling`, `doubling`, `playing`, `result`, `paused`, `stopped`), and a pause `reason` such as `unknown_popup`, `unknown_scene`, `app_switch`, `lobby_return`, or `history_mismatch`; a paused result never clicks through the scene. Mid-hand DouZero failures do not pause: they come back as `douzeroOff` while the tool plays on. In human mode never bid, double, or play by tapping the table yourself: those decisions belong to the tool. When human mode returns `paused`, do not call `start` or `play`. Read the current screenshot and call `resume` with a complete snapshot, except after `lobby_return` or `app_switch`: do not resume those. If a human-mode result is not paused but has `douzeroOff` (such as `missed_play`, `unread_left`, `unread_hand`, or `joined_mid_game`), a play could not be read, or the tool joined the hand after bidding, and DouZero cannot follow this hand: the tool cancels the game's autoplay (托管) if it is on and finishes the hand itself with simple rules (pass on your teammate's play, the game's 提示 against an opponent, lead the smallest card) and goes back to DouZero at the next deal. The same happens when our own decision fails mid-hand: a hand drift, a DouZero error or bad reply, an empty answer on a lead, a card that cannot be picked, or a play or pass the game refuses also finishes the rest of the hand with those simple rules instead of pausing, and the result reports the failure as `douzeroOff`. Do not play yourself; call `wait` if you need to. `resume` accepts a complete snapshot with required nested fields `hand`, `bottom`, `history`, `phase`, and `seat`: `hand` is the exact current hand, `bottom` has exactly three visible cards, `history` is the ordered visible plays with empty entries for passes, `phase` is one of `call`, `double`, `lead`, or `follow`, and `seat` is one of `landlord`, `landlord_up`, or `landlord_down`. Recovery validates card conservation, history, phase, and current actor, exposes the recovered decision without tapping, and the next automatic action checks the visible hand, bottom, history, seat, and phase before clicking.
In single-player mode the card play can be left to the DouZero service: call action `start` with `advisor` (the service URL the user gave you) on the 闯关模式 page of single-player mode (单机模式 → 更多玩法 → 闯关), where it taps 报名 or 继续闯关 (never 放弃闯关), at a 闯关 table, where it taps nothing (a hand already half played is left to you, reported as `douzeroOff`), on the 胜利 / 失败 screen after a 闯关 hand, where it taps the yellow 继续, or on the classic single-player result screen, where it taps the wide yellow 再来一局. In the lobby of single-player mode (单机模式 shown above the quick start), and on the single-player 经典模式 room list, it taps the bottom-right 快速开始, which opens a classic single-player game that stakes coins; on the room list it never taps a room card, because the game picks the room for the current coins. Anywhere else, or when the lobby is not in single-player mode, it refuses. From then on the tool bids by itself (three points when DouZero rates the hand as winnable as landlord, otherwise no bid) and every play turn is made by DouZero while the tool waits; the result reports `autoPlayed`. Pass `hands` (1-10) to play that many hands in a row: after each 闯关 hand the tool taps the yellow 继续 on the 胜利 or 失败 screen by itself and goes on, and the result reports `handsWon` and `handsLost`; after each classic game it taps the wide yellow 再来一局 on the result and reports `handsPlayed`; if a 当前金币可前往更高级场次 popup covers the result, the tool closes it with its 确定 itself, so do not tap that popup yourself. When coins run low the game opens the relief popup instead: tap only its free 直接领取, never 双倍领取 (video), then call `start` again from the lobby. It returns to you when the hands are done or on anything it does not recognise, such as an ad: close the ad with its ✕ (never its download or open button), then call `start` again. On a 胜利 or 失败 screen always call `start` instead of tapping: never tap 离开（保留成绩）, 放弃闯关, or a button with a video icon (免扣积分, 双倍领取). `douzeroOffs` lists the hands already settled in this call where DouZero stopped, each with `hand` (1-based within this `start` run) and `reason`; `douzeroOff` is only the hand still being played. If the result has `douzeroOff`, DouZero could not follow this game: in the classic single-player room the tool finishes the rest of that game itself with simple rules (pass on your teammate's play, the game's 提示 against an opponent, lead the smallest card) without pausing, and DouZero takes over again at the next deal; on 闯关 the play turns come back to you as below until the next game, starting with the turn on screen. Going back to the lobby ends single-player mode: call `start` again from the 闯关 page. After you close a popup over a 闯关 table, any call picks single-player mode up again.
Phases and the move to send: `call` (bid): action `call` with `level` 0 to pass, or 1-3 points. `follow` (someone played; you may beat it or pass): action `play`, `pass` or `hint`. `lead` (you must play something): action `play` or `hint`. `result` (game over): action `again` for the next game, or finish. `other`: nothing recognised; no taps were made, look at the screenshot and use the usual phone tools or call with action `wait`.
For `play`, read your hand along the bottom of the screenshot left to right and send all of it in `hand`, then the cards to play in `play`. Ranks are `3`-`10`, `J`, `Q`, `K`, `A`, `2`, `SJ` (black joker), `BJ` (red joker); suits do not matter. `hand` must have exactly `handCount` cards, which is how misreads are caught. `hint` lets the game pick: when following it plays something that beats the table, and when leading it plays your smallest card. It does not know teams, so as a farmer pass instead of beating cards your fellow farmer played, and use `play` when you know a better move.
If a play is refused the result says `rejected`; correct it once, after that the tool passes or plays your smallest card by itself so the clock does not run out.
