Play 斗地主经典版 fast, one decision at a time. Only works while that game is on screen. One call does your move and then waits on the phone, without the model, until the next decision is yours. The result gives `phase`, `handCount` and the screenshot of the new decision. Each turn has a 15 second clock, so decide at once and do not call other tools during a game.
In single-player mode the card play can be left to the DouZero service: call action `start` with `advisor` (the service URL the user gave you) on the 闯关模式 page of single-player mode (单机模式 → 更多玩法 → 闯关), where it taps 报名 or 继续闯关 (never 放弃闯关), or at a 闯关 table, where it taps nothing (a hand already half played is left to you, reported as `douzeroOff`). In the lobby it refuses with `use_stage_mode`: the quick start costs coins and opens the relief popup. Anywhere else, or when the lobby is not in single-player mode, it refuses. From then on the tool bids by itself from the hand's strength and every play turn is made by DouZero while the tool waits; the result reports `autoPlayed`. Pass `hands` (1-10) to play that many 闯关 hands in a row: after a won hand the tool taps the yellow 继续 on the 胜利 screen by itself and goes on, and the result reports `handsWon`. It returns to you when the hands are done, after a lost hand (look at the screenshot; never tap a button with a video icon), or on anything it does not recognise. If the result has `douzeroOff`, DouZero could not follow this game and the play turns come back to you as below until the next game. Going back to the lobby, or using any other phone tool on the screen (closing a popup included), ends single-player mode: go to the lobby and call `start` again.
Phases and the move to send: `call` (bid): action `call` with `level` 0 to pass, or 1-3 points. `follow` (someone played; you may beat it or pass): action `play`, `pass` or `hint`. `lead` (you must play something): action `play` or `hint`. `result` (game over): action `again` for the next game, or finish. `other`: nothing recognised; no taps were made, look at the screenshot and use the usual phone tools or call with action `wait`.
For `play`, read your hand along the bottom of the screenshot left to right and send all of it in `hand`, then the cards to play in `play`. Ranks are `3`-`10`, `J`, `Q`, `K`, `A`, `2`, `SJ` (black joker), `BJ` (red joker); suits do not matter. `hand` must have exactly `handCount` cards, which is how misreads are caught. `hint` is the fastest choice when you have no strong preference: when following the game picks a legal play, and when leading it plays your smallest card.
If a play is refused the result says `rejected`; correct it once, after that the tool passes or plays your smallest card by itself so the clock does not run out.
