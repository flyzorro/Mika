Play 斗地主经典版 fast, one decision at a time. Only works while that game is on screen. One call does your move and then waits on the phone, without the model, until the next decision is yours. The result gives `phase`, `handCount` and the screenshot of the new decision. Each turn has a 15 second clock, so decide at once and do not call other tools during a game. `start` keeps the existing single-player behavior when `mode` is omitted or `single`; use `mode=human` explicitly for a human game, always with `advisor`. In human mode, pass `auto=true` to make `start` continuously drive call, double, lead, follow, pass, and DouZero play decisions inside the phone tool until a pause, result, or unsupported scene; do not wait for the model between decisions. Without `auto=true`, `start` only attaches to the current table for inspection or recovery. In an existing human session, `start` is a recovery/idempotent call and must not reset the current game history. `stop` is idempotent and blocks later automatic work until a new `start`. `wait` drives automatic human-mode call, double, lead, follow, and pass decisions until the next visible decision, pause, result, or unsupported scene. Results return `phase`, `handCount`, `state` (`calling`, `doubling`, `playing`, `result`, `paused`, `stopped`), and a pause `reason` such as `history_mismatch`, `hand_drift`, `service_unavailable`, `rejected_action`, `unknown_popup`, `unknown_scene`, `app_switch`, or `lobby_return`; a paused result never clicks through the scene. When human mode returns `paused` or `douzeroOff`, do not call `start` or `play`. Read the current screenshot and call `resume` with a complete snapshot, except after `lobby_return` or `app_switch`: those end the game, so do not resume it. `resume` accepts a complete snapshot with required nested fields `hand`, `bottom`, `history`, `phase`, and `seat`: `hand` is the exact current hand, `bottom` has exactly three visible cards, `history` is the ordered visible plays with empty entries for passes, `phase` is one of `call`, `double`, `lead`, or `follow`, and `seat` is one of `landlord`, `landlord_up`, or `landlord_down`. Recovery validates card conservation, history, phase, and current actor, exposes the recovered decision without tapping, and the next automatic action checks the visible hand, bottom, history, seat, and phase before clicking.
In single-player mode the card play can be left to the DouZero service: call action `start` with `advisor` (the service URL the user gave you) on the 闯关模式 page of single-player mode (单机模式 → 更多玩法 → 闯关), where it taps 报名 or 继续闯关 (never 放弃闯关), at a 闯关 table, where it taps nothing (a hand already half played is left to you, reported as `douzeroOff`), or on the 胜利 / 失败 screen after a 闯关 hand, where it taps the yellow 继续. In the lobby of single-player mode (单机模式 shown above the quick start) it taps the quick start, which opens a classic single-player game that stakes coins. Anywhere else, or when the lobby is not in single-player mode, it refuses. From then on the tool bids by itself (three points when DouZero rates the hand as winnable as landlord, otherwise no bid) and every play turn is made by DouZero while the tool waits; the result reports `autoPlayed`. Pass `hands` (1-10) to play that many hands in a row: after each 闯关 hand the tool taps the yellow 继续 on the 胜利 or 失败 screen by itself and goes on, and the result reports `handsWon` and `handsLost`; after each classic game it taps the wide yellow 再来一局 on the result and reports `handsPlayed`. When coins run low the game opens the relief popup instead: tap only its free 直接领取, never 双倍领取 (video), then call `start` again from the lobby. It returns to you when the hands are done or on anything it does not recognise, such as an ad: close the ad with its ✕ (never its download or open button), then call `start` again. On a 胜利 or 失败 screen always call `start` instead of tapping: never tap 离开（保留成绩）, 放弃闯关, or a button with a video icon (免扣积分, 双倍领取). If the result has `douzeroOff`, DouZero could not follow this game and the play turns come back to you as below until the next game, starting with the turn on screen. Going back to the lobby ends single-player mode: call `start` again from the 闯关 page. After you close a popup over a 闯关 table, any call picks single-player mode up again.
Phases and the move to send: `call` (bid): action `call` with `level` 0 to pass, or 1-3 points. `follow` (someone played; you may beat it or pass): action `play`, `pass` or `hint`. `lead` (you must play something): action `play` or `hint`. `result` (game over): action `again` for the next game, or finish. `other`: nothing recognised; no taps were made, look at the screenshot and use the usual phone tools or call with action `wait`.
For `play`, read your hand along the bottom of the screenshot left to right and send all of it in `hand`, then the cards to play in `play`. Ranks are `3`-`10`, `J`, `Q`, `K`, `A`, `2`, `SJ` (black joker), `BJ` (red joker); suits do not matter. `hand` must have exactly `handCount` cards, which is how misreads are caught. `hint` lets the game pick: when following it plays something that beats the table, and when leading it plays your smallest card. It does not know teams, so as a farmer pass instead of beating cards your fellow farmer played, and use `play` when you know a better move.
If a play is refused the result says `rejected`; correct it once, after that the tool passes or plays your smallest card by itself so the clock does not run out.
